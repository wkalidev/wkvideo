Malicious Browser Extensions With 2 Million Installs Steal Session Cookies

Cybersecurity researchers have discovered a set of malicious browser extensions with a combined 2 million installs that steal session cookies from logged-in websites.

The extensions posed as PDF converters and ad blockers and worked as advertised, which helped them stay installed.

Users who installed any of the extensions are recommended to remove them immediately and sign out of all sessions on accounts they logged into.
